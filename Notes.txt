Notes - 


- A streak group is a time-boxed container (e.g., 30 or 40 days) that multiple people join and run together.

- Each member sets and works on their own goals (they don’t collaborate on a single goal).

- Members log their own progress daily, and those logs/track records are visible to everyone in that group for accountability.

- The group mainly aligns timing and visibility; goals remain personal and owned by the individual.
